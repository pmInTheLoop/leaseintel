# Business opportunities and selection

Two rounds of evaluation on 11 Nov 2026, both against the assignment's hard rules: Claude does the thinking and writing through MCP, the app stores and edits real records, a person can change them, it is live, and there is no AI inside the app.

| Idea | Weighted score /100 | Why not chosen |
|---|---|---|
| Tickmark: audit document requests between CA firms and clients | 79.2 | Built and tested as a fallback; lower willingness to pay (price-sensitive CA firms) and weaker link to Volopay |
| Collections desk (receivables follow-ups) | 72.4 | Crowded and commoditised (EnKash, Zoho, many others) |
| Month-end close checklist | 66.0 | Internal only, weaker Claude use |

| Vendor onboarding desk | 65.2 | Overlaps AP tools' vendor management |
| RenewalDesk (SaaS renewals) | 57.6 | Overlaps Volopay subscription management |

TitleOS and ParcelIQ fail on data access (state land records; zoning and GIS data). PropertyOS fails on willingness to pay (good landlord software is free; owners pay for services). Buildability is the runner-up.

## Why LeaseIntel
1. **Claude does real work:** reading a long legal document and quoting the clause behind every number.
2. **The app has an obvious reason to exist:** deadlines years away, verified by a named person, shared with people not in the chat.
3. **Money is at stake for a business buyer:** rent is one of the largest fixed costs of every location.
4. **Close to Volopay without copying it:** rent and deposits are recurring vendor payments; a verified rent schedule is exactly what a spend platform's bill pay needs.

The full evaluation, with competitors and sources, is in the "Property concepts evaluation: Volopay assignment" doc.

## What would change my mind
If finance leads at 10 to 60 location chains say their leases are already well tracked, or that their real pain is Ind AS 116 month-end entries rather than dates, the first version should start from accounting, or Buildability becomes the better bet.
