# Customer validation plan

**Status on 11 Nov 2026: no customer conversations have taken place.** Nothing in these documents is a customer quote, a pilot or willingness-to-pay evidence. Price and segment are hypotheses from desk research.

## Who to talk to
Finance controller or head of expansion at Indian chains with 10 to 60 rented locations: cafes and QSRs, salons, clinics, pharmacies, coaching centres, cloud kitchens. Start in Bengaluru, Pune and Mumbai.

## How to reach them (first two weeks)
1. LinkedIn search for "finance controller" or "head of expansion" at chains of that size; a short note offering the free lease health check.
2. CA firms that do Ind AS 116 work for such chains, asking for two introductions each.
3. Founder communities and the Volopay customer base, if a partnership conversation happens.

Target: 5 conversations before any further building, 10 within two weeks.

## The ten questions (same order every time)
1. How many leases do you manage today?
2. Where are the key terms stored?
3. How do you track renewals and escalations?
4. What happens when a lease is amended?
5. Have you ever missed a contractual deadline? What did it cost?
6. How much time goes into reading or reviewing leases each month?
7. Who owns the lease data: finance, legal, expansion, or the store team?
8. What software do you use for this now?
9. Would you pay ₹399 per location per month for this? If not, what would you pay?
10. What would stop you from using it?

## How answers are recorded
One row per conversation: date, company size (locations), role, and each answer in their words. Each row is tagged **Observation** (what they said or showed) or **Assumption** (our reading of it). No quote is used in a pitch without the person's permission.

## What would change the plan
| If we hear | Then |
|---|---|
| Most chains keep fewer than 10 leases | Raise the minimum segment size or target landlords and CA firms instead |
| Deadlines are tracked fine in Excel and nobody has missed one | The pain is weaker than assumed; test the rent-schedule and invoice-check angle |
| Accounting (Ind AS 116) is the first ask | Build the schedule export for auditors before more deadline features |
| ₹399 is too high for small chains | Test a per-workspace price for under 10 locations |
| Security review blocks adoption | Move OAuth and data-residency work ahead of features |
